1 · The parser

Ticket: ttnsy/what-it-shows#1

The shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on #4471 writes the day first, so a date with a day of 12 or less comes out with day and month swapped. This is what #4471 describes, and the queued fix is for this problem.

2 · The booking-v3 hint

Ticket: ttnsy/what-it-shows#2

Tenants on booking-v3 print MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Their people type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The result looks the same as problem 1, but the parser is not involved: the date is already wrong when the form reads it. The parser fix does not touch it. The fix is the hint, or the form’s read order.

Intake tagged 41 reports as hint. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, not from the tenant and the form, so that tag does not count this problem. The count above comes from the tenant’s form and the channel the report came in by.

3 · The one-day move

Ticket: ttnsy/what-it-shows#3

These reports do not describe a swap. The date keeps its month and moves one day: “Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday”, and one meeting showed as Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto. That is a time-zone or offset problem in how a date becomes an invite. The desk already noted these reports (desk notes, oneDayMove). The desk files them under the parser by tenant, so the 379 parser reports include these 5. They need their own ticket, because a parser fix will not change them.

  • The ticket export has 427 rows, and some reports appear twice because the export’s pages overlap. One id is one report: 412 reports.
  • The cause comes from the tenant and the form, as in causes.ts, not from the subject or from intake’s tag. All 412 reports came in under 12 different subjects. A subject is not a cause.
